Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Ink Heist (called “Çiz & Çal” in Turkish) is a mobile game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what data the game uses and why. Questions: mirzaumt@gmail.com.

In short: no ads, no tracking or analytics tools, no data selling, no free-text chat, and we never ask for your name or email address.

What we collect and why

DataWhat it isWhy
Game account IDA random ID the app creates on your device, and the account ID our server assigns.To save your progress.
Apple or Google sign-in (optional)An account identifier from Apple or Google. We do not ask for your name or email address.To keep your progress on every device.
Age groupYou enter your birth year on the device. Only the age group (under 13, 13–17 or 18+) is sent. The year never leaves your device.To give each age group the right features and limits.

Legal bases (EU/UK GDPR and Turkish KVKK): running the game you asked for (contract); security, anti-cheat and fixing problems (legitimate interests); notifications (your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings or in your phone's settings); keeping purchase records (legal obligation).

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not offer free-text chat. Players can only send preset emotes.

Who we share data with

  • Apple and Google, for sign-in, in-app purchases and push notifications. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs our servers and processes data only on our behalf.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Your opponent sees your nickname, avatar, looks and level during a match. Nothing else.

Children

We ask for a birth year with a neutral screen. Players under 13 get a limited account: no push notifications, no social features, and purchases are turned off unless a parent unlocks them. Purchases are off by default for everyone under 18 and have a monthly limit. A parent can ask us to delete a child's data at any time by email.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, progress, wallet and notification registration. We keep purchase transaction records for as long as needed to prevent fraud, handle refunds and meet tax and legal duties. Server logs are kept for a limited time. If you play as a guest and delete the app, the ID on your device is gone and that progress can no longer be recovered.

How to delete your account: Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to how we use it. Email mirzaumt@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where data is processed

Our servers and service providers may be in a country other than yours. When data leaves your region, we use the safeguards the law requires.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, such as restricted access and keeping secret keys out of our code.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
