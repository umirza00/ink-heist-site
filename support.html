Common questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Open Profile → Account and sign in with Apple or Google. Then sign in with the same account on your new phone. Guest progress is saved on one device only.

Am I playing against bots?

PvP matches are real players only. Bots appear only in Practice vs Bots, and they are always labeled BOT.

A purchase didn't arrive or I want a refund.

Close and reopen the game first; purchases are delivered when the store confirms them. Refunds are handled by the store: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history. If it still looks wrong, email us.

How do I turn off notifications?

In the game: Settings → Reminders or Match calls. You can also turn them off in your phone's settings.

Can I chat with other players?

No. To keep the game safe, there is no free-text chat. You can send preset emotes in a match (and turn rival emotes off in Settings), and players 13 and older can send preset quick messages like “GG!” or “Wanna play?” to friends.

How do I add a friend?

Open Friends in the main menu. Share your 6-letter friend code, type a friend's code, or add someone from Recent opponents. You can remove or block anyone at any time. Friends are available from age 13.
