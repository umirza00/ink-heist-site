Delete your account

Ink Heist (Çiz & Çal)

In the game

  1. Open Settings from the main menu.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then tap again to confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. This cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email mirzaumt@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion”. Include your nickname with its #tag and, if you used it, whether you signed in with Apple or Google. We may ask a question to confirm the account is yours. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account, nickname, profile and avatar
  • Progress, stats, unlocks, cosmetics, coins and gems
  • Your notification registration
  • Your friend list, friend code, quick messages and gifts

What we keep

Purchase transaction records (product, date, store transaction ID), for as long as needed to prevent fraud, handle refunds and meet tax and legal duties. Server logs are kept for a limited time. Gems and items in a deleted account cannot be restored or refunded by us.
