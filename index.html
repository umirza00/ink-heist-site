How it plays

Draw

Your ink is a road for your thieves and a wall for your rival's. Plan a route, block theirs.

Lasso

Circle a rival thief carrying gold with your ink. Lasso! The gold comes back.

Steal

Grab the gold bags that drop in the middle. Most gold when time runs out wins.

In the game

Fair by design

  • Real money only buys looks. Never power.
  • No paid loot boxes. You always see what you get.
  • Bots are always labeled BOT. PvP is real players only.
  • No free-text chat. Preset emotes and quick messages only.
  • The server decides every match result.
  • Free to play.
